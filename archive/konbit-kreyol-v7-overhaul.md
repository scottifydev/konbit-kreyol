## 1. The Copy Law (kid surfaces — lintable)

The voice contract stands: **dry, wry, teen-respecting; Strava, not Duolingo; identity-affirming; non-punitive.** These rules operationalize it for every string the boys see — including all future game narration and scene text. "Kid surface" = anything rendered outside the dev panel and the printable parent brief.

1. **No spec plumbing on kid surfaces.** Any rendered string containing `§`, "Real build", "prototype:", "audit", feed-mix percentages, or engine taxonomy (*ledger, receptive, productive, tier, band, SRS, retrieval, judgment-first, synthetic, native pass, lint, diagnostic, persistence, localStorage*) fails lint. Scott's info goes behind the dev panel; Manman's goes on the parent surface.
2. **Never name the fear.** *Failure, wrong, error, penalty, mock, lose, test* don't render in kid chrome. Safety is demonstrated by mechanics (additive chalk, sunset retries, padon patches), never announced as a denial. A reassurance repeated more than once per app is functioning as a threat — "Nobody loses" appears exactly once, at the battle-screen intro.
3. **No adjectives about the person; no number comparable across brothers on a shared surface.** Bands become course sizes ("600-word track"), visible only in each boy's own Settings. Profile doors carry name + role only. Shared boards show per-climber Kle 77 (an honest individual goal), the shared battle score, and one identical shared-stars bar in both rows; Streak/Pts live in each boy's own masthead. Row order is fixed to leg order, never sorted.
4. **Talk to the kid, never about him.** Second person or imperative; "the boys", "per boy", "each boy" fail lint on kid strings.
5. **Simplify, never falsify.** A rewrite may omit machinery but must preserve the observable contract: tapped words come back, misses set what gets taught first, the tip is genuinely required, XP is effort not mastery, padons are finite and counted. Refuter kills enforced: no "by ear" labels on tasks with no audio; no combined bars masking individual goals; empty audio slots say "coming", never pretend to exist.
6. **Requirements are enforced by buttons, not parentheses.** "(required)" and statute citations are gone; the CTA disables until the condition is met and the empty-state toast explains in one warm clause.
7. **Instructions: verb first, payoff second, ≤14 words, one imperative per line, no parentheticals. Buttons ≤4 words and name the act** ("Stamp it done", "Pass the rope →"), never the admin ("Mark", "Run", "Redo") and never a policy ("15-min minimum").
8. **Deadlines invite, they never loom.** Units, not weeks, everywhere including the masthead. Targets phrase as targets, not due-dates.
9. **Honesty without surveillance framing.** The kid is told what a tap writes — as his tool, never as the system's trick. "Retrieval hidden in the scroll" never renders, even though it's the true design.
10. **Metaphor budget (refuter finding).** Pre-pivot the app spoke: rope/climb, ticket, broadsheet, shop (FÈMEN), ladder. The game pivot re-centers everything on **the campaign** (rope/climb absorbed into it); ticket, dispatch/broadsheet, shop, and ladder survive as period-compatible flavors. Gym vocabulary ("rep") and a second "summit" are banned; new copy may not introduce further metaphors without recording it here.
11. **Vary the rhythm (refuter finding).** The "fact — warm clause" em-dash construction is the house move, not the house sentence. No two adjacent strings on a screen may share it.
12. **Locks never render.** Gated content shows the rung above you ("Sixto — next rung"), no padlock glyphs (also enforces the chrome emoji ban).
13. **Kreyòl is the home voice.** An ht string may never be colder, flatter, or less funny than its English twin; where the ht is warmer ("Ann monte", "bay lòd nan kizin nan"), the English gets raised. Claude coins nothing: doubtful words ship English with a Manman ticket. Every new/changed ht re-derives tw and enters her review queue — a copy fix can never bypass the flip gate or the native gate.

**House reference lines** (the standard all new copy is measured against): "Your ear already knows the answer. Pick what sounds right." · "Pass the rope →" · "The mountain waits for tomorrow." · "3 reactions done. Come back tomorrow — the shop reopens with fresh posts." · "She rates comprehension, not the food."

## 3. Language-law amendments

**3.1 Scope amendment (change-controlled; Manman reviews the batch before it enters the scope).** The HANDOFF abolished the name exemption but never added the name vocabulary, leaving 18+ UI entries with un-derivable tw values. Add, mirroring the konbit/padon precedent and spec §4's own U1 lists:
- **U1:** fil, fokis, misyon, pale, ekri, koute, gade, jodi a, demen, w, m
- **U2:** mòn, pwodiksyon, anrejistrè, kle
- **U3:** legim

**3.2 Expression ruling (recorded):** tw derivation matches **exact scope ids only** — no derivational credit (reyaji does not legalize reyaksyon) and no expression-membership credit (tèt chaje does not legalize tèt).

**3.3 tw recomputations** (lawful after 3.1): Fil la→3 (contains determiner *la*, taught U3), Fokis→1, Misyon→1, Mòn→2, Pwodiksyon→2, Anrejistrè→2, Kle 77→2, Pale→1, Ekri→1, MISYON FINI→1, MWEN DI L→2, microdone→2 (was an unlawful 5), Chat/Slang tags→8 (were an unlawful 1), pwomode→8 (was an aspirational 6), step_fil→8 (*reyaksyon* unscoped), any string containing an unscoped word→8.

**3.4 Manman review queue (nothing renders as gospel until her pass).** Every ht string remains Claude-authored. Specifically escalated: retired "Make misyon fini"; retired "jijman anvan"; "Paj devan" (offer "Premye paj"); "Endèks" (keep unless she objects — broadsheet voice); "Nechèl medya"; "Relè" (eye-collides with *rele*; offer "Youn apre lòt"); "badj pataje"; "Seri"; "Grenpe" (agent noun is likely *grenpè*); "revi/REVI" (retired from the badge; offer *repase*); "Nòt …" school-grade register (offer *pwen*, *zetwal*); "Mwen konprann" (new gotit ht, tw 2); greeting register ruling ("Sa k ap fèt" from an app to her sons — her call; fallback "Kòman ou ye"); production prompts ("Louvri dlo a" vs "louvri tiyo a" — family idiolect wins); post bios ("gwo pale" → offer *voye monte*/*gwo koze*; "vwa fanmi" → offer *vwa fanmi an*/*vwa lakay*); "Kapstòn" (she names the event — it's a lodyans evening); every Kanpay place/person spelling (see history brief flags); the seven surface-name glosses entering scope. ## 4. Design re-cut: "Daylight Broadsheet" (constitution v7 deltas)

**Verdict (3 judges, unanimous):** v6-4's failure was execution density, not identity — re-cut, don't replace. Both competing directions (league-light white-card; transit wayfinding) passed the teen test but failed the culture gate ("could ship unchanged as a generic Spanish app"). Broadsheet keeps what makes it *this family's app* — cooled paper, flat flag paint, the slate teaching board, the panorama, serif proverbs with the gold bar — and deletes every craft-project tell. Target read: **L'Équipe front page in our colors, not Etsy.** The game pivot strengthens the direction: dispatches, proclamations, and war bulletins are natively broadsheet forms.

**4.1 What dies (each a named teen-veto tell in the v6-4 CSS):** rotated stamps (also violated the constitution's own no-rotation ban) · perforated tikè chips · chamfered nameplate · pinstripe insets everywhere (survives on the gold mission poster only) · 5px offset shadows (the Gumroad-neobrutalism template Cycle 3 warned about) · Alfa Slab One (**Archivo Black**, already the wired fallback, becomes the display face — retiring the è/ò/à verification risk) · the 11px/.18em micro-label rash · borders-on-everything (border budget: 2px ink on slate board, inputs, hold-button, stamps only; everything else = 1px hairlines + air) · emoji avatar chips (monograms; emoji stay inside post bodies where register is native) · the red kaye margin spine (collided with red = Isaac) · the chalk tray · the `.qp` display-face-on-Kreyòl violation (quiz prompts move to Instrument Sans 600).

**4.2 Tokens.** paper `#f6f3ea` · well `#ece8d9` · hairline `#d8d2bf` (pre-authorized darken to `#cfc8b2` if it vanishes on the family monitor) · ink `#181d2b` · ink-2 `#5c5a4e` · hues unchanged (cobalt `#1a3fc4`/deep `#122d8f`, red `#d02c22`/deep `#9c1f18`, sun `#f2b02c`/deep `#c78d1a`, palm `#2e7d4f`, slate `#22303a` + well `#2c3d49`, chalk = paper, chalk-dim `#93a7b4`). No gradients, no tints, no radius. CTA depth = `0 4px 0 <deep hue>` press edge, `:active` translates down 3px. One `--shadow-overlay` allowed on exactly two floating surfaces: toast and long-press tooltip.

**4.3 Type.** Display **Archivo Black** — D1 48 / D2 30 / D3 22, ≤3 display instances per screen, never Kreyòl body. **Instrument Sans:** Kreyòl content 21px/1.6, body 16, UI 14, labels 12/700/.08em rationed ≤2 per section. **Instrument Serif italic** ≥19px for oral literature only, 4px gold bar left.

**4.4 Grafts adopted from the losing directions (judge-mandated):**
- **Scoreboard header rhythm** on every screen: one kicker · D1 title · right-aligned live tabular stat · closed by the screen's single 3px ink rule.
- **77-tick ruler** replaces the Kle 77 dashboard ring (both losers independently killed the ring as the last fitness-app cliché): one 4px tick per word, sun = solid, hairline = remaining; hero fraction beside it. Masthead keeps the small arc gauge.
- **Liy bullets** — solid letter discs (cobalt L, red I, sun K) as the boy/team identity mark on doors, shared boards, and the panorama climbers. Feed avatars are tag-family monograms; the bullet stays a team mark, not a system.
- **Counter tick** — stat numerals change via a 120ms vertical slide.
- **Per-screen failable checklist:** a screen ships with ≥1 painted moment and ≥1 display-scale element, or it has failed (the guard against de-boxed drift into quiet-generic); border budget and label ration restated as pass/fail items; fixed shared-board row order stated in a code comment.
- **Edge states as schedule, not scolding:** FÈMEN = flat painted shop sign; mic-denied = numbered steps on the slate; failed battle = sunset panorama + plain text, no well box.

**4.5 Kept, unamended:** zero gradients · one value per hue · semantic color law (cobalt=Leo, red=Isaac+alerts, sun=shared/tappable, palm=success, slate=instruction) · slate board non-punitive additive chalk (circle the right answer, neutral strike on yours, never red X) · gloss sweep + flipnew de-conflation · front-page IA · panorama SVG (+ sunset variant) · print-shop motion verbs (stamp 120 / deal 160 / wipe 250; reduced-motion instant) · pointer events · the entire language-law engine.
